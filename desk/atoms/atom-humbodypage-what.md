# HumBodyPage

## Answer

HumBodyPage is the current default L1 page mounted by the app. It builds HUM-specific graph views across the modes `structure`, `body`, `routine`, `trace`, and `compare`, manages draft graphs keyed by mode/routine/trace, and renders the shared `GraphEditor` with HUM overlays and copy. It is the active page-level orchestration path in today's app.
