# StaticFixtures

## Answer

StaticFixtures are the frozen graph payloads baked into the frontend bundle. Today that is `features/antonia-flow/antonia-fixture.generated.json` (the `?src=fixture` fallback of `AntoniaFlowPage`, generated by `scripts/generate-antonia-fixture.mjs`). They are development-time stand-ins for a live backend, not a real source of record.
